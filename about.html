about

For both of us, exercise and nutrition were already part of daily life. Our interest in medicine got us thinking about the people who could use some help making those habits their own.

We want people living with chronic illness to have someone they can ask, “Where do I start?” Our volunteers draw on their education in movement, nutrition, and health to help patients take that first step.

A better quality of life looks different for everyone. For one person, it might mean getting through more of the day with fewer symptoms. For another, it might mean trusting themselves to try again after a difficult week.

Our first goal is to connect Lansing-area patients and student volunteers. A few hours together each week leaves room for both parts of the program—the exercise itself and a conversation about how it’s going.

Students, put what you’re learning to work for one person. Patients, get guidance on a health goal that matters to you. Partners, help make that support available between medical visits.
